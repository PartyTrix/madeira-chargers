      EV charging in Madeira

      Madeira Chargers shows every public electric car charger in Madeira and Porto Santo on one map, with an estimated all-in price for each: the operator's fee, the energy price in Miio, grid access and 22% VAT. Filter for fast 50 kW+ chargers, sort by cheapest, fastest or nearest, and tap to navigate with Google Maps, Apple Maps or Waze. Chargers in the Azores and mainland Portugal are on the map too.

      How much does it cost to charge an EV in Madeira?

      Most public chargers in Madeira cost roughly €0.45 to €1.25 per kWh all-in, most under €1, including the operator fee, your app's energy price, grid access and 22% VAT. The map shows an estimated total for every charger, coloured from cheap to expensive.

      How do I pay at EV chargers in Madeira?

      Madeira's public chargers are on the MOBI.E network. Start and pay with a MOBI.E app such as Miio, or a MOBI.E card. In practice the chargers in Madeira do not take bank cards directly.

      Where are the fast chargers in Madeira?

      Tap “50 kW+” to show only fast DC chargers. Most are along the south coast around Funchal, Câmara de Lobos, Caniço and Machico, with a few in the north and on Porto Santo.

      Can I find chargers along my route?

      Yes. Type a destination in “Where to?” and the map shows the route from your location and every charger within 1 km of the road, in the order you pass them.
