EV charging in Portugal

Charge Portugal shows public electric car chargers across Portugal on one map: about 8,600 sites on the mainland plus Madeira and the Azores from MOBI.E's official data, and Tesla Superchargers and other networks from OpenStreetMap. Each MOBI.E charger that publishes a price gets an estimated total, worked out with Miio's tariff: the operator's fee plus Miio's energy price, grid access and VAT. Your own app may charge a slightly different total. See which plugs are available right now, filter by speed, plug type, card payment or motorway service areas, plan charging stops for your car's range, and tap to navigate with Google Maps, Apple Maps or Waze.

How much does it cost to charge an EV in Portugal?

On the mainland most public chargers cost roughly €0.65 to €1.20 per kWh in total, about €0.85 for a typical one, including the operator fee, the energy price, grid access and 23% VAT. Fast 50 kW+ chargers are usually at the top of that range. The map shows an estimated total for every charger that publishes a price, worked out with Miio's tariff and coloured from cheap to expensive for its region. Other apps can cost a little more or less.

How do I pay at EV chargers in Portugal as a visitor?

You don't need to live in Portugal to charge, but how you pay depends on the network and the charger. Most public chargers are on the MOBI.E network, where you start and pay with the app or card of a MOBI.E electricity supplier, such as Miio. Install it, add a payment card and check it works before your trip. Some chargers, mostly newer fast ones on the mainland, also let you pay on the spot with a bank card or a QR code, no app needed. Tesla Superchargers and other networks outside MOBI.E use their own app. Each charger on this map shows what its operator publishes; check the charger and the final price before you start. MOBI.E explains both ways in its guide for EV drivers.

Where are the fast chargers in Portugal?

Tap “50 kW+” to show only fast DC chargers. There are about 3,500 fast charging sites on the mainland, densest around Lisbon and Porto, with more than 200 at motorway service areas.

Can I find chargers along my route?

Yes. Type a destination in “Where to?” and the map shows the route from your location and the chargers within 1 km of the road, in the order you pass them. Handy for Lisbon to Porto or a drive down to the Algarve.
