EV charging in Portugal

Charge Portugal shows every public electric car charger in Portugal on one map, about 8,600 sites on the mainland plus Madeira and the Azores, with an estimated all-in price for each: the operator's fee, the energy price in Miio, grid access and VAT. Filter for fast 50 kW+ chargers, sort by cheapest, fastest or nearest, find chargers along your route and tap to navigate with Google Maps, Apple Maps or Waze.

How much does it cost to charge an EV in Portugal?

On the mainland most public chargers cost roughly €0.65 to €1.20 per kWh all-in, about €0.85 for a typical one, including the operator fee, your app's energy price, grid access and 23% VAT. Fast 50 kW+ chargers are usually at the top of that range. The map shows an estimated total for every charger, coloured from cheap to expensive for its region.

How do I pay at EV chargers in Portugal?

Almost every public charger in Portugal is on the MOBI.E network. Start and pay with a MOBI.E app such as Miio, or a MOBI.E card; one app works at every operator. Some newer fast chargers on the mainland also take a bank card at the charger.

Where are the fast chargers in Portugal?

Tap “50 kW+” to show only fast DC chargers. There are about 3,500 fast charging sites on the mainland, densest around Lisbon and Porto, with more than 200 at motorway service areas.

Can I find chargers along my route?

Yes. Type a destination in “Where to?” and the map shows the route from your location and every charger within 1 km of the road, in the order you pass them. Handy for Lisbon to Porto or a drive down to the Algarve.
