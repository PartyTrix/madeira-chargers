EV charging in France

EV Charge France shows every public electric car charger in mainland France and Corsica on one map, about 46,000 sites, with the price per kWh you pay without a subscription, incl. VAT. Where a charger doesn't publish its own price, the map shows its network's usual price with a ~. See which plugs are free right now, filter by speed, plug type, card payment or motorway service areas, plan charging stops for your car's range, and tap to navigate with Google Maps, Apple Maps or Waze.

How much does it cost to charge an EV in France?

Without a subscription, most fast chargers in France cost about €0.40 to €0.65 per kWh including VAT, and motorway service areas are usually at the top of that range, around €0.60 to €0.70. Slower AC chargers in towns often cost €0.20 to €0.50 per kWh, but some add a fee per minute. The map shows a price for every charger that publishes one, plus the usual price of the big networks, coloured from cheap to expensive.

How do I pay at EV chargers in France without a subscription?

Newer fast chargers must take a bank card, and most others let you pay on the spot by scanning a QR code. Each charger on the map says whether it takes a card. Charging cards and apps such as Chargemap or Freshmile work at most chargers too, sometimes at a different price. Tesla Superchargers are started in the Tesla app.

Where are the fast chargers on French motorways?

Tap “50 kW+” and choose “Motorway service areas” under Filters. Most motorway service areas (aires) have fast chargers from TotalEnergies, Ionity, Electra, Fastned, Engie Vianeo or Tesla, and supermarkets such as Lidl and Leclerc are often cheaper just off the motorway.

Can I plan charging stops for a road trip in France?

Yes. Type a destination in “Where to?”, enter your car's range, and the map shows every charger within 1 km of the road in the order you pass them, with suggested fast-charging stops that pick the cheapest charger near the end of each leg.
