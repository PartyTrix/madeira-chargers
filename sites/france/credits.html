Chargers, prices and live status: France's national charging-point base (IRVE, transport.data.gouv.fr, Licence Ouverte 2.0), updated nightly; plug status every 15 minutes. Prices are what operators publish for paying without a subscription, incl. VAT, as one price per kWh for a 20 kWh top-up. Prices marked ~ are a network's usual price from public price surveys (bornes-electrique.fr, MeilleureCharge), not the charger's own. Check the charger or app before charging.
